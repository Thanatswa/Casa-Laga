About Us

Casa Gala has been a beloved neighborhood gem since 1998, bringing the warmth of Portuguese hospitality and the soul of Italian cuisine to the heart of Linmeyer. Founded by the Gala family, our restaurant was born from a passion for bringing people together around a table filled with bold flavors and genuine care.

Every dish we serve tells a story from our flame-grilled peri-peri chicken, marinated for 24 hours in a secret family spice blend, to our wood-fired pizzas crafted with imported Italian flour and topped with the freshest local ingredients.

What makes us special? It's the little things: the owner greeting regulars by name, the smoky aroma drifting from our wood-fired oven, and the unmistakable taste of food made with love. At Casa Gala, you're not just a customer you're family.
